Core AI

Genetic Algorithm — Schedule Optimizer

①
Initialize
100 random chromosomes (trip arrays)
→
②
Fitness
Score based on conflicts, balance, coverage
→
③
Selection
Tournament k=3
→
④
Crossover
Two-point gene swap
→
⑤
Mutate
15% rate: swap, shift, remove
→
⑥
Best Schedule
300 gens, 10% elitism

⚠️ Penalties

  • Driver > 8hrs → -10/hr
  • Bus > 12 trips → -5/trip
  • Time conflict → -15
  • Workload imbalance → -2×variance

✅ Rewards

  • Fleet utilization → +10×rate
  • Peak hour coverage → +15×ratio
  • Balanced workload → higher score
  • Output saved to DB with metrics
